Hande Erçel

About Hande Erçel

Hande Erçel is a Turkish actress and model. She was born on November 24, 1993, in Bandırma, Turkey. She is known for her roles in popular Turkish television series. Her well-known projects include Güneşin Kızları, Aşk Laftan Anlamaz, and Sen Çal Kapımı.

Career Highlights

  1. 2012 - Won the Miss Turkey title.
  2. 2015 - Gained recognition with Güneşin Kızları.
  3. 2016 - Starred in Aşk Laftan Anlamaz.
  4. 2019 - Appeared in the series Halka.
  5. 2020 - Played Eda Yıldız in Sen Çal Kapımı.

Contributions

  • Acting in Turkish television series
  • Working in the fashion and modeling industry
  • Representing Turkish entertainment internationally
  • Taking part in different television and film projects
